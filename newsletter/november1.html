A Blessing for the Week

“For you created my inmost being;
you knit me together in my mother’s womb.
I praise you because I am fearfully and wonderfully made;
your works are wonderful, I know that full well.”

— Psalm 139:13–14

How graciously God has prepared every step of our lives—even before we were created—and how faithfully He walks with us in every season, gently reminding us of the depth of His love. The Lord has wonderful things prepared for us; He longs to draw near, to shepherd our hearts, and to use our lives for His purposes.

Allow the Holy Spirit to inspire and fill you, and come ready to share this amazing love with our families and children.
