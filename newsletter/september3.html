A Blessing for the Week

“For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith — and this is not from yourselves,
it is the gift of God — not by works, so that no one can boast.”

— Ephesians 2:8–9

Fall is right at the door, and what better way to step into the season than celebrating Community Church’s 44th anniversary. Let’s pause and remember all the gifts God has poured out since this church first came together in prayer — new lives in Christ, new friendships, new ministries, new connections, and a deepening walk with the Holy Spirit.

This Sunday, come ready to celebrate with the families and children. Bring your joy, bring your energy, and let’s share the love of God with our community.

Here are some important details for this weekend.

Don’t Miss It

Huddles

Mornings are better when we start them together. Come to the huddle — it’s where we gather in prayer for one another, connect, and share what’s on our hearts. This is a place to be heard and to hear without judgment, and to help each other grow in faith and strength in the Holy Spirit. Don’t carry it alone. Show up, and let’s begin the day side by side.

PS: Nursery, Wonder, and Discover will join together for Huddles. On the first weekend, the Huddle will use the CKids ALL TEAM Huddle link. All other Huddles will continue using the CKids Early Childhood Huddle link.
