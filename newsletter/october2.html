A Blessing for the Week

“You, Lord, are forgiving and good,
abounding in love to all who call to you.”

— Psalm 86:5

As October moves along, we can feel the end of the year drawing closer, and that can feel a little unsettling when time seems to fly. Yet God’s grace is perfect. Even in the middle of chaotic weeks or difficult seasons, He is in control, already preparing a good ending.

Even when the days feel full and the calendar moves faster than we expected, we are not left to carry the season alone. God is still writing the story, steadying our steps, and making room for joy in the middle of the rush. So we can slow down long enough to notice His kindness, trust Him with what is still unfinished, and keep pouring love into the people He has placed in front of us.

Don’t Miss It

Huddles

On Sunday mornings we gather in prayer and relationship, walking together in Jesus’ mission before we serve the kids. Huddle is a simple, important start to the day—a few minutes to pray, encourage one another, and get our hearts ready. Don’t miss the chance to belong, to connect, and to grow in faith with your church family, whether you join in person or online. Come as you are. There is a place for you, and we are stronger when we begin the morning together.

PS: Nursery, Wonder, and Discover will join for Huddles. On the first weekend, the Huddle will use the CKids ALL TEAM Huddle link. All other Huddles will continue using the CKids Early Childhood Huddle link.

We would love to see you there!
