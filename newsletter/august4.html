A Blessing for the Week

“Trust in him at all times, you people; pour out your hearts to him,
for God is our refuge.”

— Psalm 62:8

As August ends, let’s get ready for a new school year and a new ministry year that is about to begin. Let’s prepare our hearts for this new season and with open hearts love and encourage the new preschoolers to learn and enjoy all the goodness of God.

We want to thank you for completing another year with us and for being ready to love as God loves us in this new season. Your presence makes a difference in the lives of the children and families, and you are truly a gift to our team.

Let’s welcome each child and family who comes through the doors this Sunday with a joyful spirit and share kindness and love with them. Here are some important details for this weekend.
