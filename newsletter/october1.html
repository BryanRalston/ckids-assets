A Blessing for the Week

“Above all else, guard your heart,
for everything you do flows from it.”

— Proverbs 4:23

As we keep learning with ATM how to see Jesus’ messages in so many different stories, let’s take those teachings to heart and live them out. Our Creator has given us so much to absorb—and so much to pass on.

Let’s be ready to love, to be kind, to serve, and to share the gospel with our little ones. Together we can help others experience the same amazing grace the Lord has poured into our lives.

At the Movies · This Weekend

Communication during the ATM Series

After last Sunday’s larger number of children and families, we’ll use the same drop-off and pick-up method from Easter this weekend to keep things flowing smoothly. Drop children off at the side door in Discover; pick-up will be at the hall doors.

In the last 5–8 minutes of class, let’s try a short show or cartoon so the children can sit quietly — that helps us receive the next group and lets families pick up the children who are finishing.

Thank you for serving so faithfully in Jesus’s house. We are grateful to do this together.
