A Blessing for the Week

“For the Spirit that God has given us does not make us timid;
instead, his Spirit fills us with power, love, and self-control.”

— 2 Timothy 1:7

As we arrive at the last weekend of November, we find ourselves in a season of change—and of great gratitude. A new year is about to begin for our church, and together we will learn, read, and grow deeper in God’s Word as we strengthen our relationship with Him.

Our church and all of our leaders are so thankful for a team like you. Each of you has a heart that leans toward Jesus’ ministry and a desire to impact the next generation. What a gift you are!

This Thanksgiving let’s remember the greatest gift of all—Jesus and His sacrifice for us—and the many ways God continues to bless us. May we gather with hearts full of thanksgiving, love, and kindness, ready to share that same love with the children and families who walk through our doors.

Thank you for serving so faithfully. We’re grateful for you!

Don’t Miss It

Huddles

We want to encourage every team member to join us for huddle! This is such an important time for us. It’s where we share our burdens, pray for one another, walk together, grow closer as friends, and live out true community in Christ.

Starting now, we will no longer have online huddles. All huddles will be in-person only, beginning at 8:20 AM.

Please make every effort to be there. You are not alone—we need each other, and this time strengthens us as a team so we can better serve the children and families God brings through our doors.

We can’t wait to see you and start the morning together!

PS: Nursery, Wonder, and Discover will join for Huddles. On the first weekend, the Huddle will use the CKids ALL TEAM Huddle link. All other Huddles will continue using the CKids Early Childhood Huddle link.
